Miniature passive pressure-relief valve

Superpressure balloons heat in sunlight and can exceed their envelope limit. This valve vents helium passively above a set pressure and reseals as it falls — with only a few newtons of actuation force available at low-kPa differentials.

Linearity, six preloads
