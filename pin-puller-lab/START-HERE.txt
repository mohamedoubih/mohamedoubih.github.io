PIN PULLER LAB

1. Extract the ZIP (on Windows: right-click > Extract All).
2. Open the extracted pin-puller-lab folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open the app in your browser.

Keep all files in the same folder. No installation or local server is needed.
The animation works offline. The original patent links need internet access.

Use Play release, the stage buttons, or the slider to explore the movement.
Click a coloured part for its explanation.

Based on US5771742A, Figure 1 (TiNi Alloy Company).
Educational schematic; not a verified Dcubed internal design or a physics simulation.
